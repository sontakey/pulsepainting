# Pulse Painting screenshots

These are direct browser captures of the current local landscape release candidate,
including the accepted Quiet Lake paint treatment. They replace the earlier
manuscript screenshots. They do not establish a production deployment; see
[HANDOFF.md](../../HANDOFF.md) for release status.

Captured from the actual static `dist/` build at `http://127.0.0.1:64314/` between
`2026-10-23T03:45:21.438Z` and `2026-10-23T03:48:44.272Z`
(October 22, 2026 in America/Chicago). The source revision before this documentation
update was `163329204fd95c80c1eb869d29cf403836c22693`.

## Capture method

- Chrome `155.0.8059.85`, controlled through Playwright 1.63.0 on macOS.
- The three phone screens use iPhone 13 browser emulation at 390 × 844 CSS pixels.
  Desktop uses 1440 × 1000; the additional narrow desktop QR view uses 634 × 1014.
  Every capture uses device scale factor 1 and reduced motion, with fonts loaded.
- Direct browser JPEG output at quality 85, limited to the viewport. No screenshot
  pixels, interface text, metric values or QR elements were edited after capture.
- Ready and desktop are ordinary page renders. The desktop background is the
  actual Quiet Lake gallery example, generated by the app renderer from the
  public fixture with seed 39293 and 148.9 seconds of accepted waveform.
- Capture and report use a 1× wall-clock replay of
  `test/fixtures/iphone-200s.json` through the built signal engine, real monitor
  window adapter and application callbacks. Only camera I/O is mocked. The empty
  live-preview thumbnail is expected. Scene family and seed are fixed to
  `quiet-lake` and `39293` for repeatable artwork.
- The capture image was taken after 50.008 active measurement seconds and 68.924
  seconds of fixture replay. Its real countdown is **2:11**; its displayed pulse,
  RMSSD and SDNN are **67 bpm, 42 ms and 37 ms**. At that point the painting contains
  39.683 seconds of accepted waveform.
- The session completed automatically after 180.079 active measurement seconds.
  The report contains 168.733 seconds of accepted waveform (displayed as 2 min
  49 sec), and computes **71 bpm, 54.5 ms RMSSD and 51.5 ms SDNN**. The report capture
  waits for the actual artwork export to become ready. The replay consumed
  11,102 of the original 11,127 fixture samples; no repeated tail was needed.
- These are public-fixture results, not new physical-phone measurements,
  physiological-accuracy evidence, native share-sheet acceptance or a performance
  benchmark. No physical camera or native sharing action was used.

The screenshot run completed without page errors. Its reproducible capture script
and raw evidence remain in ignored `.dream-loop/release-readme/`; they are separate
from the full WebKit interruption-recovery test.
